The footer band

Mistral's signature six-stop stripe. It runs full-width above the footer — six horizontal rows with hard edges, stacked from band-1 (cream) at the top to band-6 (red) at the bottom.

Six discrete tokens · stacked rows, not a smooth gradient

band-1 · #ffe295 · hsl(45, 100%, 88%)
band-2 · #ffd900 · hsl(51, 100%, 50%)
band-3 · #ffae00 · hsl(41, 100%, 50%)
band-4 · #ff8205 · hsl(30, 100%, 51%)
band-5 · #fa520f · hsl(17, 96%, 52%)
band-6 · #e00400 · hsl(1, 100%, 44%)

The six stops map to --mistral-footer-band-1 through --mistral-footer-band-6. They are not a smooth gradient — each row is an equal-height slice with a hard edge. That's what makes the band feel printed (a flag, a thermal strip) rather than rendered (a smooth CSS gradient).

Typography

Arial carries the entire system (977 uses) — display, body, UI. No serif. The monos Pixelbasel and FragmentMono ("font-vibe") are bespoke, falling back to ui-monospace here. All headings are weight 400 — size and color carry the hierarchy, not boldness.
